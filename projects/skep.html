Overview

A complete employment platform

Skep is a cross-platform job-search and employment app built around France's RSA employment objective. It connects candidates with matched job offers, tracks their applications and goals, and links them with advisors and employers through a built-in professional network — all in one place.

I built the app end-to-end in Flutter, from concept to live release on both the App Store and Play Store, on a scalable Clean Architecture with Riverpod and Dio.

The Challenge & Goals

The Challenge

Job seekers — especially those within support programs — need one place to find relevant offers, track their applications and objectives, manage their profile, and stay connected with advisors and employers. The app had to deliver this as a polished, enterprise-ready product across both platforms, matching detailed UI designs exactly.

The Goals

  • Matched job offers with compatibility scoring
  • Application tracking & objective dashboard
  • Professional circle of advisors & employers
  • Social login & profile management
  • In-app messaging & secure sessions
Key Features

What Skep does

Job Offers & Matching

Browse job offers with a compatibility score so candidates see their best-fit roles first.

Applications Dashboard

Track weekly applications and monthly objectives with a clear, visual progress dashboard.

Professional Circle

Connect with advisors, employers and partners through a built-in professional network.

Social Login

Fast, frictionless sign-in with social authentication.

Profile Management

Candidates manage their profile, details and preferences in one place.

In-App Messaging

Real-time chat between candidates, advisors and employers.

Engineering Highlight

Enterprise-ready architecture

Skep was built as an enterprise-grade product: a scalable Clean Architecture with Riverpod for state and Dio for robust API integration, secure global session management, an advanced calendar/booking system, and pixel-perfect custom UIs implemented exactly to the design specs across both platforms.

Tech & Architecture

How it's built

Built in Flutter on a modular Clean Architecture with Riverpod for state management and Dio for API integration, plus Firebase for backend services, in-app messaging and push notifications, social login and secure session handling.
