Overview

Holistic healthcare, at your doorstep

NiramayaSoul is a one-stop healthcare platform built around the belief that true wellness goes beyond the physical — integrating medical care, diagnostics and wellness services under one roof. Customers in tier 1–4 cities across Jharkhand and West Bengal can get medicines delivered, book doctor consultations, schedule lab tests and access privacy-assured wellness services — all within 24 hours, at affordable rates.

I built the NiramayaSoul mobile app from scratch in Flutter using GetX for clean, reactive state management and REST APIs for live data. Firebase handles authentication and push notifications — delivering a smooth, consistent experience across iOS and Android from a single codebase.

The Challenge & Goals

The Challenge

People in tier 2–4 cities across Jharkhand and West Bengal face significant barriers to quality healthcare — fragmented services spread across pharmacies, clinics and diagnostic labs, long wait times, and no trusted digital platform. Accessing a doctor, getting medicines or booking a lab test often means multiple trips across the city. The need was a single, affordable platform that brings all of it to their doorstep.

The Goals

  • Medicine delivery within 24 hours
  • Seamless doctor consultation booking
  • Easy diagnostic & pathology services
  • Privacy-assured wellness services
  • Affordable care for tier 1–4 cities

Tech & Architecture

How it's built

NiramayaSoul is built in Flutter for a seamless, native-quality experience on both iOS and Android from a single codebase. GetX handles state management, dependency injection and routing — keeping the app reactive and performant across its many service modules. All healthcare data — doctors, medicines, diagnostics and orders — is powered by REST APIs, while Firebase manages user authentication and push notifications.
