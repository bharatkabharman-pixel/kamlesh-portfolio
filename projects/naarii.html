Overview

A jewellery brand, in your pocket

Naarii Jewels is a dedicated mobile storefront for a silver and artificial-jewellery brand, giving customers a beautiful, app-native shopping experience. Shoppers can browse curated collections, explore detailed product pages and place orders — all within a smooth, reactive interface.

I built the Naarii Jewels app from scratch in Flutter with GetX powering clean, reactive state management — delivering a polished shopping experience on both iOS and Android from a single codebase, with Firebase handling the backend and push notifications.

The Challenge & Goals

The Challenge

Jewellery brands typically rely on physical stores or generic e-commerce websites that don't showcase their products well on mobile. Customers browsing jewellery on a small screen need high-quality visuals, easy navigation by category, and a frictionless way to discover and order — none of which a website does well as a dedicated app.

The Goals

  • Beautiful branded storefront for the jewellery brand
  • Rich product catalog with images & details
  • Browse by category & collection
  • Seamless in-app ordering
  • Push notifications for offers & order updates
Key Features

What Naarii Jewels does

Product Catalog

A rich, visually-led catalog of silver and artificial jewellery — earrings, necklaces, bangles and more.

Collections & Categories

Browse jewellery by category or curated collection — making discovery intuitive and enjoyable.

Product Details

High-quality product images, descriptions, materials and pricing — everything needed to make a confident purchase.

Cart & Ordering

Add items to cart and place orders directly in the app — quick, simple and reliable.

Wishlist

Save favourite pieces to a wishlist and come back to them whenever ready to buy.

Push Notifications

Firebase-powered alerts for new collections, exclusive offers and order status updates.

Tech & Architecture

How it's built

Naarii Jewels is built in Flutter for a consistent, pixel-perfect experience on both iOS and Android. GetX handles state management, dependency injection and routing in a lightweight, reactive way — keeping the UI snappy as users browse the catalog. Firebase powers authentication, data storage and push notifications for order updates and promotional campaigns.
