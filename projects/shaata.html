Overview

Healthcare access for the spiritual community

Shaata is a unique support platform built for Munis & Sadhus — members of the monastic community — helping them find nearby doctors, hospitals and diagnostic labs, view availability, get directions, and request consultations, all from one app.

I built the Shaata mobile app in Flutter with GetX for reactive state management, delivering a clean, map-based experience on Android.

The Challenge & Goals

The Challenge

Munis and Sadhus often travel and follow strict routines, which makes finding trusted, nearby healthcare difficult. The community had no dedicated tool to locate doctors, hospitals and labs, see what's open nearby, or request a consultation — so care was hard to coordinate.

The Goals

  • Find nearby doctors, hospitals & labs
  • Map & list discovery with distance and availability
  • One-tap navigation & calling
  • Consultation requests with status tracking
  • A clean, simple experience for all users
Key Features

What Shaata does

Find Doctors & Hospitals

Search for nearby doctors, hospitals and diagnostic labs in seconds.

Map & List View

Discover providers on an interactive map with distance and availability, or browse as a list.

Navigate & Call

One-tap directions to a provider and direct calling, right from the listing.

Consultation Requests

Request a consultation with a doctor and track its status through to completion.

Shraman Profiles

Dedicated profiles for the monastic community, keeping their details and requests in one place.

Push Notifications

Timely updates on consultation requests and responses.

Engineering Highlight

Map-based discovery, done smoothly

At the heart of Shaata is location-based discovery — plotting nearby doctors, hospitals and labs on a live map with distance, availability and one-tap navigation. GetX kept state and routing lightweight and reactive, while Firebase handled data and push notifications, keeping the experience fast and simple for every user.

Tech & Architecture

How it's built

Built in Flutter with GetX for reactive state management and routing, Firebase for backend data and push notifications, and integrated maps & location services for provider discovery and navigation.
